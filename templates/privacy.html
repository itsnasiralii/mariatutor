Privacy & safety

We operate as an introduction service for Burewala tuition requests. Parents and tutors do not see each other's direct contact details in public search or opportunity listings.

  • Tutor CNIC is used as a login input but is stored as a keyed one-way identifier. Do not reuse this password elsewhere.
  • CNIC scans and certificates are stored in private application storage and require an authorized admin permission to review.
  • Parents see broad area, class, subject and teaching mode; tutors see only limited opportunity details.
  • Admins may view personal contact details only when their Super Admin permission allows it. Important access and changes are audited.

For a real launch, publish a legally reviewed privacy notice, retention period, consent process and contact channel before collecting documents.
