Detailed Agreement Clauses & Liabilities

1 Zero-Tolerance Harassment & Child Safeguarding

Maria Tutor operates under strict conformity with the Pakistan Penal Code (PPC Sections 354, 376, 509) and The Protection Against Harassment of Women at the Workplace Act. All tutors, parents, and students must strictly honor physical and personal boundaries.

  • Physical Integrity: Tutors are prohibited from touching any student under any pretext. Corporal punishment (slapping, hitting, pinching) is completely banned and treated as criminal assault.
  • Open-Door Rule: Every tuition session involving minors must be conducted with the room door wide open or in an open, visible family area.
  • Grooming Prevention: Giving personal gifts, sharing private secrets, or developing romantic attachments is strictly illegal and subject to criminal prosecution.

2 Privacy, Digital Decorum & PECA 2016 Enforcement

Under the Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act (PECA 2016), home premises and family privacy are strictly protected:

  • Zero Unauthorized Recording: Tutors and students are forbidden from taking pictures, audio recordings, or videos inside the family home.
  • No Cyber-Stalking: Neither party may contact, follow, or stalk each other on social media (Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, Snapchat).
  • Anti-Defamation: No party may post defamatory statements, reviews, or videos online. All grievances must be routed through Maria Tutor Official Dispute Desk.

3 Anti-Bypass Policy & Fee Discipline

Maria Tutor is the official guarantor, mediator, and placement authority:

  • No Direct Private Contracting: Tutors and parents solemnly covenant never to bypass Maria Tutor to avoid commission. Any bypass attempt results in immediate legal recovery of damages, permanent CNIC blacklisting, and notification to local educational institutions.
  • Fee Schedule: Monthly tuition fees must be settled between the 1st and 5th of every month. First-month agency placement fees are governed by the active platform commission rule.
  • 15-Day Notice Period: Either party wishing to discontinue tuition must provide at least 15 calendar days advance written notice through the platform.

4 Female Tutor Dignity & Household Protocols

Female educators visiting homes in Burewala are entitled to the highest safety and social respect:

  • Respectful Household Environment: Adult male members of the household must maintain dignified decorum and not intrude into the female tutor's designated teaching space.
  • Safe Entry and Exit: Families must ensure secure, well-lit entry and exit paths.
  • No Domestic Errands: Tutors are hired purely for academic tuition; expecting them to perform household chores or babysitting is strictly prohibited.

5 Violation Severity Matrix & Legal Enforcement

Violations are classified into three legal tiers with escalating consequences:

Violation Tier Offense Nature Operational Action Legal & Financial Penalty
Tier 1: Minor Tardiness, excessive phone use, messy attire, unexcused absence. Formal warning + replacement tutor offered to family. Forfeiture of 15% placement fee on repeat offenses.
Tier 2: Policy & Ethics Covert bypass attempt, unapproved substitute, verbal disrespect, smoking. Immediate contract revocation + tuition cancellation. Permanent profile deactivation + recovery of platform damages.
Tier 3: Severe Criminal Sexual harassment, physical abuse, theft, cyber-blackmail, secret recording. Instant emergency termination + permanent blacklisting across Pakistan. Direct Police FIR (PPC 376/509, PECA 2016) + handover of CNIC & documents to law enforcement.
